Students

Find your subteam

Try more than one. Plenty of members end up wiring on Tuesday and coding on Thursday.

01

Mechanical

Design the robot in CAD, then build it: cutting, drilling and riveting aluminum and polycarbonate in the RSTA shop. You'll learn to use real tools safely.

CAD · band saw · drill press · rivets
02

Electrical

Wire the robot's nervous system: motor controllers, sensors and the power distribution that keeps it alive through a match. Clean wiring wins matches.

crimping · CAN bus · power distribution
03

Programming

Write the Java code that drives the robot, from driver controls to autonomous routines. If you've never written code, we'll teach you.

Java · WPILib · Git
04

Strategy & Operations

Scouting other teams, drive team, budget, fundraising, social media and the pit. This is the work that turns a robot into a team.

scouting · outreach · sponsorships
The season

What a year looks like

Sep – Dec

Fall training

Learn the tools, practice CAD and code, and work on small projects before the real thing.

January

Kickoff

FIRST reveals the new game. We read the rules, brainstorm and pick a design.

Jan – Mar

Build season

The busiest stretch. We meet the most often and the robot goes from sketch to machine.

Mar – Apr

Competition

New England District events, usually two per season, plus repairs in the pits between matches.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need to know anything about robots?

No. Most members join with no experience. Fall training exists so new members can learn the tools before build season.

Does it cost anything?

Sponsors and fundraising pay for robot parts and event fees. If cost is a concern for anything, like travel or gear, talk to the leadership team. We don't want money to keep anyone off the team.

How much time does it take?

It depends on the time of year. Fall is lighter. Build season (January to March) is the busiest, and competitions are full weekends. Come to what you can.

Can I join partway through the year?

Yes. The fall is the easiest time to start, but there's always something to learn and something to help with.

I'm more into design, writing or photography. Is there a place for me?

Yes. Strategy & Operations handles social media, sponsorships, branding and the pit display. We need those skills every season.
