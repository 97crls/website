Sponsors

Powered by our partners

A competition robot means metal, motors, electronics, event fees and travel. Our sponsors cover those costs so that money never keeps a CRLS student off the team.

Thank you

Our supporters

Thanks also to Cambridge Rindge and Latin School and the Rindge School of Technical Arts for our shop, tools and home base, and to the families and mentors who keep the team running.

Get involved

Become a sponsor

Sponsoring Team 97 puts your name behind hands-on engineering for public school students in Cambridge. Kendall Square, home to many of the companies our students hope to work for, is about a mile down the road.

What your support pays for

  • District event registration
  • Robot parts: motors, controllers, sensors, aluminum and polycarbonate
  • Shop tools and safety gear
  • Team shirts, travel and outreach materials

What sponsors get

  • Your logo on the robot, pit banner and team shirts
  • Recognition on this website and our Instagram
  • A robot demo at your office, if you'd like one

Invest in the herd

Every dollar goes to students and comes back out as a robot. Get in touch and we'll get back to you quickly.
