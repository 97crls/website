2026 · REBUILT™

The 2026 robot

REBUILT ("Uncover the Future") had robots collecting and scoring piles of yellow foam balls. We built a box-frame robot around rows of green compliant-wheel rollers that pull balls in and feed them through to score. A net across the top keeps them from bouncing out.

The frame is aluminum extrusion with polycarbonate and plywood panels, so we could see inside and cut new parts fast between events.
